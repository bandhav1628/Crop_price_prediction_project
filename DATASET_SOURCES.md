# Datasets for Market Price Prediction in Sustainable Agriculture

## 1. Government Agricultural Market Price Data
- **India**: [Agmarknet Portal](https://agmarknet.gov.in/)
    - Daily prices for agricultural commodities from hundreds of markets (mandis) across India.
    - CSV downloads available.
- **US**: [USDA Agricultural Marketing Service](https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news)
    - Daily, weekly, and historical price reports for crops, livestock, and more.

## 2. Weather Data
- [OpenWeatherMap API](https://openweathermap.org/api)
    - Historical and real-time weather data (temperature, rainfall, humidity).
- [NOAA Climate Data](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/cdo-web/)
    - US-focused climate and weather records.

## 3. FAO Global Price Data
- [FAOSTAT Prices](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/CP)
    - International prices, production, and trade statistics for various crops.

## 4. Indian Ministry of Agriculture
- [Data.gov.in Agriculture Resources](https://data.gov.in/catalogs/agriculture)
    - Market prices, crop yields, production statistics, and more.

## 5. Kaggle Datasets
- [Kaggle: Agricultural Commodity Prices](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ranjeetjain3/agricultural-commodity-prices-india)
- [Kaggle: Crop Production Statistics](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/atharvaingle/crop-production-statistics-india)
- [Kaggle: Vegetable Prices India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amolgupta/vegetable-prices-india)

## 6. Other Useful Sources
- [World Bank Data](https://data.worldbank.org/topic/agriculture-and-rural-development)
- [Global Open Data for Agriculture and Nutrition (GODAN)](https://www.godan.info/pages/open-data)

---

## How to Use
- Combine market price data with weather/crop yield data for better predictions.
- Use APIs for real-time data collection and model updates.
- Most sources offer CSV or API access; check documentation for usage rights and formats.
